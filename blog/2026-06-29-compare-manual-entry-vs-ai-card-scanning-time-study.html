Manual Entry vs AI Card Scanning: A Time Study for Sales Professionals

In the fast-paced world of sales and networking, the way you manage contacts can make or break your success. The traditional method of manually entering information from business cards into your CRM system is not only tedious but also prone to errors. On the other hand, Artificial Intelligence (AI) card scanning technology has emerged as a game-changer. In this blog post, we will conduct a comprehensive time study comparing manual entry and AI card scanning to highlight how DigiCards can streamline your contact management process.

Why Time Management Matters in Networking

For sales professionals, entrepreneurs, and real estate agents, time is a precious resource. According to a study by HubSpot, sales professionals spend only 36% of their time actively selling. Much of their time is wasted on administrative tasks, including data entry and contact management. By optimizing this aspect of your workflow, you can reclaim valuable minutes and focus on what truly drives your business forward—building relationships and closing deals.

Understanding Manual Entry

The Traditional Approach

Manual entry involves physically writing down or typing the information from a business card into your CRM system. While this method has been the standard for years, it presents several challenges:

- Time-Consuming: On average, entering a single business card can take anywhere from 1 to 5 minutes, depending on the complexity of the information.

  • Error-Prone: Human error is inevitable. Typos can lead to miscommunication, lost opportunities, and damaged relationships.
  • Limited Integration: Many manual entry systems lack seamless CRM integration, making it difficult to manage contacts effectively.

Real-World Example

Let’s take John, a real estate agent who attends networking events regularly. After an event, he collects 30 business cards. If we assume he spends 3 minutes on each card, that adds up to 90 minutes of tedious data entry. If he makes just one mistake per card, he risks reaching out to the wrong contacts or losing important details.

The Advantages of AI Card Scanning

What is AI Card Scanning?

AI card scanning technology uses advanced algorithms to read and transcribe the information from business cards directly into your CRM or contact management system. This innovative approach offers numerous benefits:

- Speed: Scanning a business card takes about 5 to 10 seconds. This means that John could scan 30 cards in just 5 minutes.

  • Accuracy: With AI’s ability to recognize and transcribe text, the error rate is significantly lower compared to manual entry.
  • Seamless CRM Integration: Many AI card scanning solutions, including DigiCards, offer easy integration with popular CRMs, ensuring your contact management is always up-to-date.

Real-World Example

Imagine if John used DigiCards instead. After the same networking event, he scans the 30 business cards in just 5 minutes. Not only does he save 85 minutes, but he also ensures that all data is accurate and readily available in his CRM.
