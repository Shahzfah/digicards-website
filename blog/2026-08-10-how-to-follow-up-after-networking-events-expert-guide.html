How to Follow Up After Networking Events: Expert Guide

Networking events can be a goldmine for sales professionals, entrepreneurs, and real estate agents looking to expand their connections and grow their businesses. However, the success of these events often hinges on the follow-up process. In fact, statistics indicate that nearly 80% of sales require five follow-up calls after the initial meeting. Yet, only 44% of salespeople follow up after the first touchpoint. This discrepancy highlights the importance of mastering follow-up techniques to maximize your networking efforts.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore actionable tips to effectively follow up after networking events, how to manage your contacts efficiently using a business card scanner, and the importance of CRM integration for successful contact management.

The Importance of Following Up

Before we dive into strategies, let's understand why following up is crucial. Following up after networking events does not just solidify new connections; it also reinforces your brand and establishes your credibility. Being proactive in your outreach can help you stand out from the competition, making it more likely that potential clients and partners will remember you.

Why You Shouldn’t Wait Too Long

Timing is critical in the follow-up process. According to a HubSpot study, 80% of sales require at least five follow-ups after the initial meeting. If you wait too long, you risk your new contacts forgetting who you are. Aim to follow up within 24-48 hours after the event to keep your conversation fresh in their minds.

Tips for Effective Follow-Up

1. Organize Your Contacts

After a networking event, your first step should be to organize the contacts you've made. Consider using a business card scanner like DigiCards to quickly digitize and store the information you’ve collected. This not only saves time but also reduces the chances of misplacing vital contact details.

#### Example: Imagine you collected 30 business cards at a conference. Instead of manually entering each one into your phone or CRM, you can simply scan them using DigiCards. This ensures you have accurate data without the hassle.

2. Personalize Your Message

Generic follow-up messages are easily ignored. Take the time to personalize each message by referencing something specific from your conversation. This could be a shared interest, a project they mentioned, or even a compliment about their presentation.

#### Example: "Hi [Name], it was great meeting you at the [Event Name]! I really enjoyed our discussion about [specific topic]. I'd love to continue our conversation and explore how we can collaborate."

3. Choose Your Medium Wisely

The medium you choose for follow-up communication can significantly affect how your message is received. Email is often the most professional choice, but don’t overlook social media platforms like LinkedIn. A brief message on LinkedIn can be more effective for casual connections.

#### Example: If you met someone in a more informal setting, consider sending a quick social media message. "Hi [Name]! Loved chatting about [topic] at [Event]. Let’s connect on LinkedIn!"

4. Provide Value

In your follow-up, aim to offer value to your new contact. This could be sharing a relevant article, inviting them to an upcoming event, or even introducing them to someone in your network who might benefit them.

#### Example: "Hi [Name], I came across this article on [specific topic] that I thought you might find helpful. Also, I’d love to introduce you to [Contact Name] who is working on a similar project."

5. Set a Reminder for Future Follow-ups

Following up once is great, but to build a lasting relationship, you need to stay in touch. Use your CRM integration tools to set reminders for future follow-ups. This can help you keep track of your interactions and ensure you don’t let valuable relationships slip through the cracks.

#### Example: After your initial follow-up, set a reminder to reach out in a month. A simple message like, "Hi [Name], just checking in! How are things going with [their project]?" can keep the conversation going.

6. Use CRM Tools for Efficient Contact Management

Investing in a CRM system can streamline your follow-up process. With CRM integration, you can keep all your contacts organized, track your communication history, and set follow-up reminders easily. This is especially useful for sales professionals who need to manage large volumes of contacts effectively.

#### Example: Using a CRM like Salesforce or HubSpot can help you categorize your contacts based on their interests or stages in the sales funnel. This makes it easier to tailor your communication strategy.

Best Practices for Networking Follow-Ups

1. Follow Up on Promises

If you promised to send information or make an introduction during your conversation, make sure to follow through. This builds trust and demonstrates your reliability.

2. Be Consistent

Consistency is key to building relationships. Establish a schedule for your follow-ups, whether it’s weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. This keeps you on the radar without being overbearing.

3. Monitor Your Success

Keep track of your follow-up efforts and their outcomes. This can help you identify what strategies work best for you and refine your approach over time.
