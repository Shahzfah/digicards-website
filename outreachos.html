Common Questions

Questions brokers ask us

We get the same three questions from almost every broker. Here's the honest answer to each.

"What if my agents don't use it?"
They don't have to. OutreachOS runs at the brokerage level — you're the only user. No agent logins, no training, no adoption problem. It runs in the background and you approve what goes out.
"Will these emails look like AI spam?"
You see every email before it goes out. If it doesn't sound like you, you change it or skip it. Outreach emails are plain text — no banners, no templates. They look like you personally wrote them, because you approved them.
"I tried GoHighLevel. It took 6 months to set up."
GoHighLevel is infrastructure — a blank canvas for agencies to build on. OutreachOS is a done-for-you output machine. Your first campaign goes live within 48 hours. No sequences to build, no blank templates to fill.
"What if it gets my domain blacklisted?"
Every email is CAN-SPAM compliant by default — physical address, functional unsubscribe, honest sender info, all built in. Daily send limits are capped at 20 emails to protect your domain reputation. We don't blast; we warm up gradually and stay well inside safe thresholds.
"How do I know it's actually working?"
Your dashboard shows replies, open rates, and posts published — not vanity metrics. But the real frame is this: $199/month produces more public-facing content than most brokers create in a year. You're paying to look like you have a marketing team.
